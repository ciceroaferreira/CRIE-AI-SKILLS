# US holiday, retail and awareness calendar

Pick only the dates that fit the business and its customers; nobody needs to post on all of them. For any year
not listed, run `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/us_dates.py --year YYYY [--brazil] [--lang pt]` instead of guessing.
Dates below for 2026–2027 were computed by that script; lunar dates (marked ≈) come from published calendars and
can shift by a day.

Fixed dates that move the day off: when a federal holiday falls on Saturday it is observed Friday; on Sunday,
Monday. 2026: July 4 (Sat) observed Fri 07/03. 2027: Juneteenth (Sat) observed Fri 06/18; July 4 (Sun) observed
Mon 07/05; Christmas (Sat) observed Fri 12/24; New Year's Day 2028 (Sat) observed Fri 12/31/2027. Banks and post
offices close on observed days — plan delivery and payment messages around them.

## Month by month

**January** — New Year's Day · "New year, new you" (fitness, organizing, cleaning, finance) · MLK Day (service,
not sales) · Q4 estimated tax due 01/15 · Veganuary / Dry January (food and drink)
**February** — Black History Month · Super Bowl (food orders, watch parties; see trademark note) · Valentine's Day ·
Presidents' Day sales · Lunar New Year · Ramadan may start · American Heart Month (wear red, first Friday)
**March** — Women's History Month · St. Patrick's Day · daylight saving starts (2nd Sun) · spring cleaning ·
March Madness (NCAA basketball, mid-March to early April) · partnership/S-corp returns due 03/15 (next business
day if weekend: 03/16/2026)
**April** — Easter (2026 in April, 2027 in March) · Tax Day 04/15 (2026 Wed, 2027 Thu; Q1 estimate due same day) ·
Earth Day 04/22 · Administrative Professionals' Day (04/22/2026, 04/21/2027) · Autism Acceptance Month · prom season
**May** — AAPI Heritage Month · Mental Health Awareness Month · National Small Business Week · Teacher
Appreciation Week (1st full week) · Cinco de Mayo 05/05 · Mother's Day · graduations · Memorial Day (start of summer)
**June** — Pride Month · Juneteenth 06/19 (federal) · Father's Day · weddings · Q2 estimate due 06/15 · end of
school year
**July** — July 4th · summer sales · Amazon Prime Day (Amazon announces each year; small shops run counter-promos) ·
back-to-school ads start mid-July in the South
**August** — Back to school (South and Florida start early-to-mid August; Northeast after Labor Day) · state sales
tax holidays (many states, e.g., Florida runs one in August, Texas and Massachusetts on an August weekend — check the
state revenue department for dates and eligible items) · National Black Business Month
**September** — Labor Day (end of summer sales) · Grandparents Day · Hispanic Heritage Month 09/15–10/15 · Q3
estimate due 09/15 · fall menus · Rosh Hashanah (some years in October)
**October** — Breast Cancer Awareness Month (pink) · Halloween · Indigenous Peoples' Day · extended individual
returns due 10/15 · Diwali (2027) · fall festivals, pumpkin everything
**November** — Native American Heritage Month · Movember · daylight saving ends (1st Sun) · Veterans Day 11/11 ·
Diwali (2026) · Thanksgiving week: pre-orders, catering, "thankful" posts · Black Friday → Cyber Monday ·
Small Business Saturday (strongest date for local shops) · Giving Tuesday
**December** — Hanukkah · holiday parties and gift cards · Super Saturday · carrier shipping cutoffs (USPS, UPS,
FedEx publish them each year, usually mid-December) · Christmas Eve/Day · Kwanzaa 12/26–01/01 · New Year's Eve ·
year-in-review posts

## Awareness months (verify the organizer before using a logo or name)

Black History Month (Feb) · Women's History Month (Mar) · AAPI Heritage Month (May) · Mental Health Awareness Month
(May) · Pride Month (Jun) · Hispanic Heritage Month (Sep 15–Oct 15) · Breast Cancer Awareness Month (Oct) ·
Native American Heritage Month (Nov) · Financial Literacy Month (Apr) · National Homeownership Month (Jun) · Life
Insurance Awareness Month (Sep). Post only if the business has something real to say or do (feature people,
donate, educate). Pink-ribbon or Pride products: say exactly what is donated ("$2 from every box goes to X")
and donate it — vague cause marketing is a deception risk and some states regulate commercial co-ventures.

## By industry (examples)

- **Food/bakery**: Thanksgiving pie pre-orders (open early Nov, close ~3 days before), Super Bowl platters,
  Valentine's, Mother's Day brunch, graduation cakes, National Donut Day (1st Fri of June), Pi Day 03/14.
- **Cleaning/home services**: New Year organizing, spring cleaning (Mar–Apr), move-out season (May–Aug, college
  towns), pre-holiday and post-party cleaning (Nov–Dec), gift certificates for Mother's Day.
- **Beauty/med spa/salon**: Valentine's, prom and wedding season, Mother's Day gift cards, summer body (with care,
  see the advertising rules), holiday party glam, Black Friday packages (no lottery or "free procedure" bait).
- **Accountants/tax preparers**: January kickoff (W-2s and 1099s arrive by 01/31), 03/15 business returns, 04/15
  Tax Day, extension season to 10/15, quarterly estimates (04/15, 06/15, 09/15, 01/15; a deadline on a weekend or
  legal holiday moves to the next business day, e.g., 01/15/2028 → 01/18/2028), year-end planning (Nov–Dec).
- **Life insurance agents**: Life Insurance Awareness Month (September, run by the nonprofit Life Happens), new
  baby/new home/wedding season (Apr–Jun), Mother's/Father's Day ("protect the family" — no fear tactics),
  year-end reviews. **Medicare** agents: Annual Enrollment Period Oct 15–Dec 7 (marketing allowed from Oct 1),
  Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Jan 1–Mar 31, Turning-65 birthdays all year (CMS rules apply, see the
  advertising rules). **Health (ACA) agents**: Open Enrollment starts Nov 1; the end date (Dec 15 for Jan 1
  coverage, up to Jan 15 or later in some states) changes with federal rules and varies by state exchange — check
  HealthCare.gov or the state marketplace every year before posting a deadline.
- **Financial services (advisors, tax, lenders, credit)**: tax season (late Jan–Apr 15), Financial Literacy Month
  (April), America Saves Week (late February), National Homeownership Month (June) for mortgage lenders and
  realtors, back-to-school budgeting (Aug), open enrollment for benefits (Oct–Nov), year-end tax planning and RMDs
  (Nov–Dec, confirm with the CPA/advisor). Every post goes through compliance first (see the advertising rules).
- **Builders/contractors/remodelers**: spring home-improvement season (Mar–Jun; book estimates in Jan–Feb), storm
  prep (Atlantic hurricane season Jun 1–Nov 30 in FL/Gulf/East Coast), summer roofing/decks/pools, fall
  winterizing and gutters (Sep–Nov), National Homeownership Month (June), holiday-ready remodels (book by Sep).
  Before/after project photos are great content — real projects, with the homeowner's permission, no address.
- **Immigration/translation services**: tax season (ITIN, translated documents), back to school (school records),
  news-driven posts only after the attorney reviews them.
- **Real estate**: spring market, Fair Housing Month (April). Never describe who a home is "perfect for" by race,
  religion, family status, disability, national origin or sex.
